# p0-v4 capsule generation record

All four capsule masters were generated as complete, integrated scenes with the pet references supplied directly to ImageGen. No pet was composited onto a separately generated background afterward.

## Shared direction

- Preserve the supplied Gulugulu logo and the recognizable pet designs.
- Paint pets, furniture, environment, lighting, reflections, and contact shadows as one coherent scene.
- No sticker outlines, cutout halos, pasted-character borders, rectangular seams, or isolated top/bottom panels.
- Colorful, warm, readable at Steam thumbnail size; clear logo safe area.
